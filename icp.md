# who
students in a Python fundamentals course or bootcamp.
early in it: sessions 1 to 5, basics to functional programming.
have a laptop or phone, 10 minutes a day, no setup skills.

# trigger: when they need us
"the exam is next week and I've forgotten session 2"
"I followed the class but can't write it myself"
"I keep falling for the same trick questions"

# where they are
their course's WhatsApp or Discord group.
classmates sharing notes and notebooks.
LinkedIn and Instagram posts about learning to code.

# not a fit
experienced developers.
people wanting data science, pandas or ML.
people who need a certificate.
anyone wanting full projects instead of daily practice.
